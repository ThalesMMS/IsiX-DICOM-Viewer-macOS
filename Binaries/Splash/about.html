About IsiX DICOM Viewer

What It Is

IsiX DICOM Viewer is a free, open source medical image viewer for macOS. It is a fork of Horos, which is based upon OsiriXTM and other open source medical imaging libraries. IsiX DICOM Viewer is made available under the GNU Lesser General Public License, Version 3 (LGPL-3.0).

IsiX DICOM Viewer is not made, sponsored or endorsed by the Horos Project, Purview or Pixmeo. Horos, Horos Cloud and OsiriX are trademarks of their respective owners and are named here only to say where the code comes from. The IsiX DICOM Viewer name and icon are not covered by the license of the code.
